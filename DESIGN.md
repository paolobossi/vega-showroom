# Vega · UI system

The rules the app's stylesheet follows (`docs/index.html`, tokens at the top of `<style>`). Change a value here and in `:root`, never in a single rule.

## Type
Four sizes, each with one job. Hierarchy beyond these comes from weight, colour, case and space, not new sizes.

| Token | Size | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `--fs-caption` | 12px | Labels (uppercase), tags and pills, meta lines, helper text, chips |
| `--fs-body` | 15px | Everything you read or tap: rows, buttons, values, callouts, card names |
| `--fs-heading` | 17px | Section headings (Now, Today, Ready-to-wear), sheet and nav titles, client names, form inputs (17px also stops iOS zooming into a field) |
| `--fs-title` | 28px | Page titles, the product name, big numbers (quantities, PIN keys, KPI totals) |

- Weights: 400 regular, 600 semibold (labels, values, buttons), 700 bold (titles and primary buttons). Nothing in between.
- Line height: `--lh-tight` 1.2 for headings and titles, `--lh-body` 1.4 for text.

## Colour
- **Structural:** `--bg` (white canvas), `--surface` (light grey cards and groups, with squircle corners and no outline), `--surface-2` (pressed and secondary fills), `--card` (white: Collection cards and the selected segment), `--line` (dividers inside a box). Home appointments sit on the white page split by a light 1px rule (55% of `--line`) from the page margin to the right edge, like order lines, with no box or shadow; the ongoing one under Now too, marked only by its green Ongoing tag. The Profile rows are separate grey rows with a 2px gap instead of a divider (the budget, Guidelines and product details rows are the exception: white boxes with a grey outline, see below; names and amounts 14px regular, with a bold Total row last; 14px is a third exception to the four sizes).
- **Text greys:** `--ink` for text, `--muted` for secondary text that must be read (4.5:1 on the canvas), `--faint` only for large text, placeholders and non-text marks (3:1).
- **Primary action:** black (`--ink` with `--bg` text). One per screen: Add to order, Finalize order, Send to commercial team, Continue. Checking a client in is green instead (`--good`), always with the same check icon (18px, 2.2 stroke), 46px, 15px semibold: Check in, Check in again, Start session. Choose a client stays black with no icon (it opens the list, it doesn't check anyone in).
- **Quantity toasts** ("Saved · N units", "Added to order · N units") are a black card with Apple squircle corners (the `--squircle` mask at 32px, rounder than the 22px boxes; up to 340px wide) instead of a pill: the message, then the style's category budget bar: category name (12px, 75% opacity) and spend "€1.680 of €9K" (spend bold), over a 6px track (`--bg` at 20%) filled in the budget-bar colours (red #F2553F under half, yellow #F2B705 from half, green #2BBF6A once reached), live from the order, the fill easing 0.35s as it grows. A bar that just reached its budget (category or division Total, detected when the order changes) gets `.pop`: a 0.5s springy scale (1 → 1.06 → 0.98 → 1.02 → 1), on the order page and in the toast, plus a haptic: `navigator.vibrate([40, 60, 80])` where it exists (Android), otherwise two programmatic clicks on a hidden native `<input type=checkbox switch>` 140ms apart (iOS 18+ Safari gives a haptic tap for a switch); once per crossing. The bar pages have 4px of room above and below so the bounce isn't clipped. Every toast slides in (0.32s) and, after 2.2s, slides back out the way it came (0.28s, ease-in: down for the bottom ones, up for the top ones: quantity changes and Removed from order) before it is removed; a new message while one is leaving brings in a fresh toast.
- **Toasts** never repeat the client's name: "Session ended", "Checked out", "Women sent · Checked out".
- **Interactive (`--accent`, blue):** links only (and the blue status pills and info callouts). Every active or selected state is black (`--ink`): the active tab, the selected day, active filters, selected options, sizes with a quantity, focus rings (a text field in focus gets a 2px black inner outline, no halo). Text fields type at the placeholder size (the Notes box 14px, like the Budget and Guidelines rows); the viewport has maximum-scale=1 so iOS does not zoom into fields under 16px. Never a filled primary button.
- **Status:** green `--good`, amber `--warn`, red `--bad`, each with its `-bg` tint. Always with a word or icon too (Within, Over, ⚠), never colour alone. Budget spend is red under half of the budget, yellow from half, and green once it is reached or passed. The budget bars use bright tints of these (green #2BBF6A, yellow #F2B705, red #F2553F) mixed with the white page, not with grey; the figures in them are 17px bold and the category name above them is the same colour; the bars are 52px tall and the icon tile on the left is upright, 34×40 with a 17px squircle; the name sits 6px tighter to the figures, the pair centred in the bar and then nudged 1px down so it sits centred by eye. At most 3 bars show at once: with more, `BarStack` pages them 3 at a time (3 × 52px, 8px apart), a page as wide as the content, 18px between pages, scrolled sideways with mandatory snap a page at a time, the scroller bleeding into the page margins exactly to the screen edges (−17px in the order card, which has a 1px border; padding and scroll-padding the same) so the swipe runs edge to edge; the order page body has overflow-x hidden, so only the bars scroll sideways; under it the product carousel's dots, centred 10px below (6px dots at ink 25%, the current one a 16px `--ink` bar with 4px corners; ink-based so they show in dark mode; tappable, no frosted pill), and empty slots on the last page filled with `--surface` placeholder bars of the same size and squircle. "In the order" is a green ✓: a filled green round ✓ on the Collection card (about as tall as the priority tag), and on each colour chip a green outlined circle with a green ✓ (no fill), 16px like the swatch.
- **Black marks:** `--band`, black in light and dark mode (Must have tag, status bar strip, client pill). The client in session shows as a badge on the Order tab (16px tall, its number 11px regular) and as a black pill floating 8px above the tab bar, as wide as it (`SessionPill`, min 54px, fully round, soft shadow): a 9px green dot (#3CCB7F) pulsing, the client's name 15px semibold white, under it "N items" 12px at 60% white, a chevron on the right; tapping it opens the Order tab. On the Order tab (the client's name is already the page title) it slides down out of sight behind the tab bar (0.34s, fading), and slides back up on any other tab; the page leaves room for it (146px at the bottom) and bottom toasts sit above it.
- **Tab bar:** a floating white pill near the bottom (see below), the page scrolling under it; Home, Looks, Scan (middle), Collection, Order; active item black and bold on a grey capsule, others grey.

## Shape and depth
- Radius: `--r-xs` 4 (chips, bars), `--r-s` 8 (photos), `--r-m` 12 (controls, inputs, size cells), `--r-l` 16 (cards, list groups, panels), `--r-xl` 22 (sheet tops). Anything as tall as it is round (pills, round buttons) uses 999px.
- Shadows, light from above: `--shadow-raised` (selected segment), `--shadow-float` (floating buttons, toast), `--shadow-sheet` (bottom sheets). Cards use a white surface and a hairline border, not shadows.

## Space
- 2px micro grid: every padding, margin and gap is an even number of pixels.
- Page gutter 18px. Inside components: 4–8px between a label and its value, 10–14px inside cards, 16–24px between sections.

## Actions
- One primary (black, filled), secondary (white with a border), tertiary (blue text link). Two buttons side by side at the bottom of a sheet are each half the width. Finalize order is text only.
- Status tags are just the word (Ongoing has no dot in front) and no tag has a border: grey tags are `--ink` at 9% over whatever is behind, Ongoing is `--good` at 18%, so they read on white, on grey and green rows, and in dark mode. Essential and Permanent stay white on the photo.
- Every button is 46px tall and semibold (600), sheet, page and alert alike.
- The browser's `theme-color` (the strip around the notch in Safari) follows the screen: the page colour normally, #000 with the camera, and while a sheet's scrim is showing the scrim blended over the page (#98999B light, #060707 dark), so the top matches the dimmed page.
- On the Order tab, Finalize order and the tab bar share one backdrop: the button's area fades in over 36px and then runs on, at the tab bar's own 70% white, down behind the tab bar to the bottom of the screen (the tab bar's own fade is off there), so there is no gap between them.
- Tab bar: a floating pill (`.tabbar`, `--card` at 78% with a 20px blur, radius 999px, shadow 0 1px 2px / 0 6px 24px), 18px from the sides, in line with the page content, and max(10px, safe area) from the bottom, over a fade to white; the active tab sits on a capsule (`.tab-ind`, ink at 7%, radius 999px) that slides to the tapped tab (transform, 0.34s ease-out; none under prefers-reduced-motion); the active label is 600.
- Figures: plain (proportional) everywhere, never tabular: Inter's tabular 1 has a foot (a slab), so no element uses `tabular-nums` (`.num` included).
- Product photos: 18 ready-to-wear photos in `docs/img` came as portrait shots padded to a square with darker grey side bands (37–96px); the bands were filled by repeating, row by row, the background just inside the photo, so every photo's background runs edge to edge. The product page views in `docs/img/v` were not touched.
- Money: `fmt` / `fmtK` print euros with a dot for thousands and a comma for decimals (de-DE grouping, since it-IT leaves 4-digit numbers ungrouped), € in front: €18.000, €18,5K.
- Segmented controls with equal segments (`Seg full`) draw the selected pill as one `.seg-ind` indicator that slides to the chosen segment (transform, 0.32s ease-out; none under prefers-reduced-motion).
- Rows of choices of different widths slide the same way: `useSlide` measures the selected item and moves one `.slide-ind` there (left and width, 0.32s, same curve; jumps into place on first show; none under prefers-reduced-motion). Used for the Home days (black 24px squircle tile), the Collection collections (2px underline) and the Collection categories (black pill; the other pills are light grey `--surface` with no outline; category names 12px (`--fs-caption`) regular, selected or not, in a 30px pill). `useSlide` measures with getBoundingClientRect (sub-pixel), not offsetLeft / offsetWidth, which round and left the marker 1px off a regular-weight label. Each item's grey fill is its `::before`; the black marker sits above the row and holds a white copy of the row's labels (`.slide-copy`), shifted back by the same amount so it stays still while the marker moves: labels read white exactly inside the black and dark outside it, at every frame (the iOS way). The labels themselves never change colour. The selected item's grey keeps its size and goes (opacity 0, after a 0.32s delay) only once the marker covers it; an item that loses the selection gets its grey back at once. Both layers move with `left`, not `transform`: two nested composited transforms drifted apart in Chrome. When the selection changes in a row that scrolls, `useSlide` also scrolls it (smooth; instant under prefers-reduced-motion) so the tapped item's neighbour shows in full, 18px from the edge: the next one when the item is near the right edge, the one before near the left; the Home days snap to their tiles, so there the scroll stops on the first tile edge past that point. The product page's (and sizes sheet's) colour chips use `useSlide` too: the chosen colour is a black pill that slides, holding a copy of the row. Trial: the pill is the chosen colour's own swatch colour (`pillOf`: `--pill`, with `--pill-ink` #141619 when its luminance is above 0.62, else white) and its background fades over the 0.32s slide; the copy's text, check and ring use `--pill-ink`. Every swatch in a chip has a 1px ring of 40% white inside (on the pill, 45% of `--pill-ink`). In a colour chip the name (semibold) and the code (`--ink`, regular, 6px after the name; the other gaps 8px) are both 15px with a 20px line height, centred, so they share one baseline; the swatch is centred too.
- Product page photo: 3:2 (a classic photo ratio; lower than the old 5:4, not as flat as 16:9), the whole product shown on its light grey; a runway look's photo in its sheet stays 5:4, since it is cropped from a tall picture.
- Product page prices: no longer on the page; they open the details `.g-box` as two side-by-side highlight tiles (`.pd-hl`: `--surface`, 12px squircle = the box's 22px corner less its 10px inset; 10px from the box's top, left and right and 10px between the two tiles), label 12px `--muted`, price 20px bold (a fifth size, for these two figures only).
- Budget, Guidelines and the product page details sit in a `.g-box`: a grey (`--line`) squircle with 1px padding around a white squircle list, no rules between rows (Budget rows 10px apart, Guidelines rows 16px). `.g-box` and the Order tile use a 22px squircle (`--r-xl`).
- The Order tile in the client sheet is white with a 1px grey (`--line`) outline, drawn as a grey squircle around a white one so it follows the squircle in every browser. It is the one outlined box; other content boxes are grey with no outline.
- Profile circles are a soft grey gradient, lighter at the top (`--avatar-top`) and darker at the bottom (`--avatar`), with white initials (darker greys and light initials in dark mode), never flat black or white.
- Order status banners (With client, Sent, Partly sent, Confirmed) carry no colour fill: white (`--bg`) inside a `.g-box` (1px `--line` outline, 22px squircle), title 17px semibold `--ink`, the line under it 12px `--muted`; only the 38px round icon is coloured (`--warn` With client, `--accent` Sent / Partly sent, `--good` Confirmed) with a white glyph.
- Destructive actions are red outline or red text and ask first (native confirm). Leave (end the client session) is red text on a white pill with a grey outline at the top right of the order, and asks with an in-app alert (Cancel / Yes, leave). In the ongoing client's sheet on Home, Leave is the white half-width button with a grey outline and red text, next to Add products, and asks the same.
- A look's pieces use the order-line layout: no box, light 1px rule edge to edge, 72px squircle photo, wholesale price only.
- Sheet titles on the left have about 20px under the title block, matching the room above.
- Order page (trial): the budget card sits on white with no box; the order's lines are one list under the Order heading (no category headings), each line with no box, split by a light 1px grey line running edge to edge, its squircle photo 72px and flush with the heading; Edit sizes there is a white pill with a grey outline, the stepper is a grey outline pill with no fill, and units / value are 12px; figures on the lines are proportional (Inter's tabular 1 has a foot).
- Sheet titles: the close × is centred on the title's line.
- A sheet about one piece (Add to order) shows its photo left of the title: a 48px tile, 12px squircle, 12px from the title (`Sheet` `thumb` prop; a look uses its first runway photo).
- Client lists in sheets (Choose a client, Add to order): the sheet stays white; sections in the order Today, Past, Upcoming; headings 15px semibold `--muted` on the page margin, 8px above their box (no h3 margins), 32px from one box to the next heading; each section is one box like the Budget box (`.grp` grey `--line` squircle with 1px padding round a white `.grp-in` squircle) with 40px squircle corners (the boxes are tall, and at 22–28px the squircle read as pointy); 4px of padding at the top and bottom of the white box; rows at least 60px, 16px padding on the left, split by a light 1px rule (55% of `--line`) from 16px in to the box's right edge, the name 17px regular, the date under it 12px `--muted` ("Mon 9"), Today's rows too; a `--faint` chevron (Lucide chevron-right, 18px) at the end.
- Loading placeholders in the Collection follow the cards: no box, no border, a grey 24px squircle for the photo and bare grey lines for the text, flush with its edges.
- Collection cards: white, no shadow, no box of their own: the photo carries the 24px squircle (all four corners) and the details sit flush with its edges (no side padding), lines 4px apart, prices right after their labels (not right-aligned), wholesale and retail both bold; the grid has 28px between rows (8px between columns), family headings 10px above their cards; with one category picked the category heading is left out (the pill already names it), with All each category has its heading; earlier notes on the card mask follow: shaped only by the mask (border-radius 0: Safari has no corner-shape and would draw round bottom corners); photo full-bleed to the card's top and sides, soft drop shadow (on a wrapper, since the squircle mask clips a shadow on the card itself).
- Squircle corners (the `--squircle` mask) are Apple's continuous corners, as on iOS (Figma corner smoothing 60%): a circular arc eased into the straight sides by curves, so they read soft with no point; the corner runs 1.6× its arc radius (a mask width of 24px is about a 15px radius). They replaced a superellipse (n = 5) that read as pointy. Generated by a script in the 48×48 mask image (corner 20 units: arc radius 12.5). Bottom sheets (product page, Add to order, client sheet and the others; not full-screen pages) get the same corner on their two top corners only, from `--squircle-top` (bottom corners square) at 34px. They come from the mask alone: masked boxes have border-radius 0 wherever the mask is supported (a radius as well would clip the corners again, round in Safari, and eat into outlines). Squircle radii: budget bars 32px (icon tiles in them 17px), look cards 20px, Home day tiles, Home appointment list and Collection cards 24px.
- Priority tags (Must have, Essential) are small pills in regular weight (Permanent, the same white pill, only on the product page, never on a Collection card): 11px on the Collection card, 12px on the product page. Tab bar labels are 10px regular. These two are the exceptions to the four text sizes.
- Segmented controls are pills: container and selected segment fully round on the sides (999px).
- App icon (home screen, `docs/apple-touch-icon.png` 180, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`): a white four-pointed sparkle with concave sides on a black square (no rounding: the system rounds it), the star 330px of 512 so it stays inside the maskable safe zone; the splash background in the manifest is black too. Made by `tools/icon/make.mjs` (headless Chrome; the second argument sets how far the sides curve in). The links carry `?v=2` so phones fetch the new icon.
- Icons are Lucide (ISC) everywhere, drawn as lines (1.8px stroke, heavier for small marks like ✓, + and −). Two exceptions from Phosphor (MIT): Shoes is the high heel in its regular line weight (Lucide has none), thickened with a stroke so its line matches the Lucide icons beside it, and the city pin on Home is Phosphor's filled map pin (a line icon is too thin at 12px).
- Size cells (product page, sizes sheet): white vertical pills (fully round) with a 1px `--line` outline, no fills, 8px apart: size 12px regular `--muted` on top, a round 40px + (pressed: `--surface-2`), the number 22px semibold, −; every cell keeps the same grey outline (no black outline for a size with a quantity): the number is `--ink` when above 0 and ink at 22% at 0 (`.qty.zero`, like a placeholder; the one-size quantity too). Under them Copy scale, Paste scale and Clear sit centred and are always there, disabled until usable.
- Icons for + and −, never text glyphs, so they line up the same in every stepper. Steppers are a white pill with a grey outline and no fill behind − and + (order lines; the one-size quantity on the product page, a size larger).
